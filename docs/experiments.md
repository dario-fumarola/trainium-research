# The experiments and their controls

## Written calculations and supervised learning

**October 16–22 · Qwen3-1.7B-Base.** The first question was whether a model could generate a useful intermediate calculation from information available before the answer. On 384 fresh real-document arithmetic cases, supervised trace models answered 342 and 335 correctly across two seeds. Matched direct-answer models answered 275 and 285 correctly. The trace models generated about 34 additional actions per case and received more supervised target tokens. Ordinary-text loss worsened relative to the original base. [Arithmetic study and controls](https://github.com/dario-fumarola/useful-thoughts-trainium/blob/d07d89e0348cf1acd76acd1173a90dcd11facf3e/docs/EXPERIMENT_HISTORY_20261019.md)

The permutation experiment made the distinction between executing a trace and learning a better direct answerer especially clear. With generated traces the model solved 18,432/18,432 and 18,430/18,432 unseen in-range compositions. Removing the trace largely removed the benefit, and longer compositions remained difficult. On natural document QA, the two-seed study missed its inference and training criteria. Passage-selection, format and coefficient controls are recorded with the original failures. [Permutation results](https://github.com/dario-fumarola/useful-thoughts-trainium/blob/d07d89e0348cf1acd76acd1173a90dcd11facf3e/validation/permutation_program_v28_complete_20261020/REPORT.md) · [QA decisions](https://github.com/dario-fumarola/useful-thoughts-trainium/blob/d07d89e0348cf1acd76acd1173a90dcd11facf3e/validation/document_QA_v21_complete_20261019.json) · [Evidence-selection studies](https://github.com/dario-fumarola/useful-thoughts-trainium/blob/d07d89e0348cf1acd76acd1173a90dcd11facf3e/docs/archive/README_through_20261021.md)

**Would this help a reader on a typical document?** Six frozen selectors chose when to use a calculation. On 1,024 fresh calculation continuations all six improved over the base by 0.421–0.506 nats per 16-token span. Five beat every extra control. On 4,096 unenriched natural windows every selector lost, by 0.020–0.052 nats per span. A constant ensemble without calculations gained 0.0244. Selecting arithmetic cases changes the population enough to change the conclusion. [Fresh reader comparison](https://github.com/dario-fumarola/useful-thoughts-trainium/blob/d07d89e0348cf1acd76acd1173a90dcd11facf3e/validation/reader_fresh_confirmation_complete_20261021/REPORT.md)

**Would computing during training improve the weights?** The earlier scaffold experiment missed its direct-prediction criterion, and its baseline reweighted selected continuations. The follow-up trained a uniform next-token baseline. The chosen schedule improved fresh FineMath loss by 0.014481 nats/token (95% interval 0.013903–0.015058), but lost 0.008835 on educational English. Six more fits compared ordinary training, an empty-format auxiliary, and verified computation after the document. All six improved over the starting model. Computation beat format-only and lost to ordinary by 0.000944 and 0.000908 nats/token in the two orders. Independent CPU forwards preserved the direction. [Baseline](https://github.com/dario-fumarola/useful-thoughts-trainium/blob/d07d89e0348cf1acd76acd1173a90dcd11facf3e/validation/uniform_lm_baseline_complete_20261022/REPORT.md) · [Six matched fits](https://github.com/dario-fumarola/useful-thoughts-trainium/blob/d07d89e0348cf1acd76acd1173a90dcd11facf3e/validation/scaffold_learning_complete_20261022/REPORT.md) · [CPU verification](https://github.com/dario-fumarola/useful-thoughts-trainium/blob/d07d89e0348cf1acd76acd1173a90dcd11facf3e/validation/scaffold_standard_CPU_verification_20261022/REPORT.md)

**What did improve both domains?** Keeping the ordinary objective and mixing equal shares of FineMath and educational English improved fresh math by 0.011346 and 0.011204 nats/token and English by 0.008550 and 0.008756. The all-math reference still won on math alone. The overnight program reports 56.900 assigned chip-hours across its five accounted studies, including failed reservations. The twelve released models include the weaker controls. [Source mixing](https://github.com/dario-fumarola/useful-thoughts-trainium/blob/d07d89e0348cf1acd76acd1173a90dcd11facf3e/validation/uniform_mixture_complete_20261022/REPORT.md) · [Accounting and release](https://github.com/dario-fumarola/useful-thoughts-trainium/blob/d07d89e0348cf1acd76acd1173a90dcd11facf3e/docs/OVERNIGHT_RESULTS_20261022.md)

## Early versus late pauses

**October 22 · Randomly initialized Qwen3-0.6B architecture.** The plan inserted 16 unlabeled positions per 128 real tokens, either from the first update or from halfway through training. A third model used ordinary next-token prediction. Five qualification attempts consumed 11.863 assigned chip-hours. Compilation and device occupancy were observed, but the pilot closed before update-and-recovery qualification passed. There is no early-versus-late quality score. [Design](https://github.com/dario-fumarola/pause-pretraining-trainium/blob/a7d6a14c30b12c5c6a920f04c1c1b5121065afa2/docs/PAUSE_PRETRAINING_V1.md) · [Closure receipt](../evidence/pause-closure.json)

**October 22 · Separate 150M natural-text experiment.** This successor did finish. At about one billion real targets, fixed early pauses scored 2.573797 NLL against 2.519907 for ordinary training. Ordinary won in all three paired seeds and every domain. This answers the fixed-pause comparison at that exposure. The 0.6B timing question remains open. [Learning curves and costs](https://github.com/dario-fumarola/autonomous-compute-trainium/blob/15d52172a8773c905d9951641ea9c0cbc3451fd5/results/learning_curve_20261022/REPORT.md)

## Repeated layers and learned workspaces

**October 23–27 · Reusing layers.** A variable-depth model trained to run one, two or three passes improved by 0.027214 nats when evaluated at depth three versus depth one on a frozen test. Yet its two-pass loss was worse than fixed-two training and ordinary training at matched work. Later fixed recurrence beat a one-pass relative, but an eight-layer ordinary control had lower average development loss, 2.480522 versus 2.483862. The two-seed interval crossed zero. With human-written arithmetic steps progressively withheld, a recurrent model solved 4/135 held-out answers despite over 99% accuracy on a training sample. The team stopped the recurrence campaign on October 27. [Variable-depth test](https://github.com/dario-fumarola/autonomous-compute-trainium/blob/15d52172a8773c905d9951641ea9c0cbc3451fd5/results/variable_depth_20261023/test/REPORT.md) · [Eight-layer control](https://github.com/dario-fumarola/autonomous-compute-trainium/blob/15d52172a8773c905d9951641ea9c0cbc3451fd5/results/lean_baseline_controls_20261026/main/ordinary8/REPORT.md) · [Arithmetic curriculum](https://github.com/dario-fumarola/autonomous-compute-trainium/blob/15d52172a8773c905d9951641ea9c0cbc3451fd5/results/natural_chain_continuation_pilot_20261025/recovery-v2/REPORT.md) · [Change of research direction](https://github.com/dario-fumarola/autonomous-compute-trainium/blob/15d52172a8773c905d9951641ea9c0cbc3451fd5/docs/WORKSPACE_RESEARCH_CHARTER_20261027.md)

**October 27–29 · Learning what to write.** A pretrained 1.485B model generated temporary workspaces and received a predictive training signal. On 320 development anchors, ordinary continuation scored 2.387626 next-four-token NLL, versus 2.422095 for the learned writer. Giving the reader the actual future as a privileged diagnostic helped substantially. The writer could not supply an equally useful signal from the prefix. A continuous-workspace variant also trailed ordinary, by 0.004209 nats on its full-window measure. Residual-memory, probability-refinement and sparse-workspace controls tested whether the content or an extra pass accounted for gains. Replacing memory often retained most of the benefit, and the sparse model lost its matched-work comparison. [Discrete writer](https://github.com/dario-fumarola/autonomous-compute-trainium/blob/15d52172a8773c905d9951641ea9c0cbc3451fd5/results/workspace_discovery_20261027/evaluation-recovery-v1/REPORT.md) · [Continuous workspace](https://github.com/dario-fumarola/autonomous-compute-trainium/blob/15d52172a8773c905d9951641ea9c0cbc3451fd5/results/spliced_workspace_discovery_20261028/REPORT.md) · [Memory replacement](https://github.com/dario-fumarola/autonomous-compute-trainium/blob/15d52172a8773c905d9951641ea9c0cbc3451fd5/results/residual_memory_attribution_20261028/REPORT.md) · [Sparse control](https://github.com/dario-fumarola/autonomous-compute-trainium/blob/15d52172a8773c905d9951641ea9c0cbc3451fd5/results/chunked_persistent_workspace_20261029/control-completion-v1/REPORT.md)

## Persistent internal states

**October 29–30 · Training from scratch.** Here the model generated a continuous vector after each real token. Later tokens could attend to it. At 128M targets, a selected seed beat the strongest tuned ordinary control by 0.025047 nats on reused development data. Two new seeds gained 0.002267 and 0.028967, missing the prespecified replication criterion. Varying the number of internal steps also failed to produce a monotonic improvement. [Tuned comparison](https://github.com/dario-fumarola/autonomous-compute-trainium/blob/15d52172a8773c905d9951641ea9c0cbc3451fd5/results/interleaved_workspace_20261029/tied-comparison-v1/REPORT.md) · [Replication](https://github.com/dario-fumarola/autonomous-compute-trainium/blob/15d52172a8773c905d9951641ea9c0cbc3451fd5/results/interleaved_workspace_20261029/seed-replication-v1/REPORT.md) · [Step budget](https://github.com/dario-fumarola/autonomous-compute-trainium/blob/15d52172a8773c905d9951641ea9c0cbc3451fd5/results/budgeted_interleaved_20261029/learning-v1/REPORT.md)

With 1B targets per model, the latent model improved broad loss by 0.030495 nats. Removing access to its own past states raised broad loss by 0.636840. That intervention establishes dependence on the trained pathway. On the 259 source-audited arithmetic cases it correctly predicted only five answer prefixes. At matched nominal matrix work, ordinary training saw 3.783B targets and won broad loss by 0.110176 nats and arithmetic answer loss by 0.088747. Conditional intervals describe source groups for these checkpoints. Training-seed variation remains a separate uncertainty. [Equal exposure](https://github.com/dario-fumarola/autonomous-compute-trainium/blob/15d52172a8773c905d9951641ea9c0cbc3451fd5/results/latent_exposure_20261030/learning-v1/REPORT.md) · [State ablations](https://github.com/dario-fumarola/autonomous-compute-trainium/blob/15d52172a8773c905d9951641ea9c0cbc3451fd5/results/latent_exposure_20261030/content-recovery-v1/REPORT.md) · [Matched work](https://github.com/dario-fumarola/autonomous-compute-trainium/blob/15d52172a8773c905d9951641ea9c0cbc3451fd5/results/latent_exposure_20261030/work-recovery-v1/REPORT.md)

**Engineering result.** Batching gradient reduction and clipping cut a warm ordinary-model update from 0.2934 to 0.0616 seconds. Reversing the order gave 0.2884 and 0.0612 seconds. The qualification checked gradients, optimizer moments, agreement across 64 replicas and a saved next-update replay. The 4.71–4.77× speedup applies to this ordinary training graph.
